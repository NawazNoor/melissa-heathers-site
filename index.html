About Melissa

Storytelling, strategy & soul.

Melissa Heathers is an author, speaker and creative visionary, and the founder, executive producer and host of The Book Crawl. She is devoted to transforming books into platforms — and to positioning authors as thought leaders who shape culture, healing and conversation.
